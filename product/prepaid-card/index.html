Overview

This prepaid card is designed for anyone who wants a card for budgeting, gifting or online use without a credit line. All names, rates and terms shown are placeholders for this demonstration.

Features & benefits

  • Contactless payments in shops and secure checkout online.
  • Instant alerts for every transaction, with card lock in the app.
  • Manage limits and view statements from internet banking.
  • No credit check and no linked account needed.
  • Top up from any DemoBank account or at a branch.

Who can apply

  • Available to anyone who wants a card for budgeting, gifting or online use without a credit line.
  • Proof of identity and address.
  • Proof of income for credit cards.
  • A DemoBank account for debit and prepaid cards.

How long does a card take to arrive?

How do I block a lost card?

Credit cards in this demo show up to 45 interest-free days on retail purchases if the full balance is paid on time.
