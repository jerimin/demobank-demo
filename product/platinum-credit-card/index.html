Overview

This platinum credit card is designed for salaried or self-employed applicants who meet the minimum income requirement. All names, rates and terms shown are placeholders for this demonstration.

Features & benefits

  • Contactless payments in shops and secure checkout online.
  • Instant alerts for every transaction, with card lock in the app.
  • Manage limits and view statements from internet banking.
  • Up to 45 interest-free days on retail purchases.
  • Redeem points for statement credit, travel or gift vouchers.

Who can apply

  • Available to salaried or self-employed applicants who meet the minimum income requirement.
  • Proof of identity and address.
  • Proof of income for credit cards.
