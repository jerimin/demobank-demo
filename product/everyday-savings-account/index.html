Overview

This everyday savings account is designed for anyone aged 18 or over who wants a simple account with easy access to their money. All names, rates and terms shown are placeholders for this demonstration.

Features & benefits

  • Open and manage the account entirely online or in the DemoBank app.
  • Free internet and mobile banking with instant transaction alerts.
  • Transfer money within DemoBank at no charge, any time.
  • Free DemoBank debit card for the first year.
  • Interest calculated on the daily balance and paid every month.

Who can apply

  • Open to anyone aged 18 or over who wants a simple account with easy access to their money.
  • A valid photo ID and proof of address.
  • One recent passport-size photograph.
  • A tax identification number where required by law.
