Overview

This car loan is designed for salaried or self-employed applicants buying a new or reconditioned car. All names, rates and terms shown are placeholders for this demonstration.

Features & benefits

  • Clear, reducing-balance interest — you pay interest only on what you still owe.
  • Fixed monthly instalments so you can budget with confidence.
  • Part-prepayment allowed to lower your interest over time.
  • Decision and disbursement handled at your branch or online.

Who can apply

  • Open to salaried or self-employed applicants buying a new or reconditioned car.
  • Proof of identity, address and income.
  • Bank statements for the last six to twelve months.
  • Security or a guarantor where the product requires it.

Frequently asked questions

How is the monthly instalment worked out?

On a reducing balance: interest applies to the amount you still owe, and each instalment pays some interest and some principal.

Can I repay early?

Yes. Part-prepayment lowers the balance that interest is charged on. An early-settlement charge may apply, shown above.

How long does approval take?

In a real bank, typically a few working days after documents are verified. This demo approves nothing.
