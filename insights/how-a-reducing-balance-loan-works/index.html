How a reducing-balance loan actually works

On a reducing-balance loan, interest is charged only on what you still owe, so it falls as you repay.

  • Each instalment is split between interest and principal.
  • Early on, more of the instalment is interest; later, more is principal.
  • Paying a little extra early reduces the balance that interest is charged on.
  • Use the EMI calculator on the home page to try different amounts and terms.
