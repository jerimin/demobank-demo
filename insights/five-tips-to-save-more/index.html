Five simple habits that grow your savings

Small, repeatable habits beat big one-off efforts. Here are five that add up over a year.

  • Pay yourself first: move a set amount to savings on payday, before you spend.
  • Automate it so the decision is made once, not every month.
  • Keep an emergency buffer of three to six months of expenses.
  • Review subscriptions twice a year and cancel what you no longer use.
  • Set a named goal, because money with a purpose is easier to keep.
