Safe banking

How to keep your money and details safe.

DemoBank will never ask for your password

We will never ask for your full password, PIN or one-time code by phone, email or message. Anyone who does is trying to scam you.

Protect yourself

  • Keep your password, PIN and OTP to yourself — always.
  • Type the web address yourself and check it before you log in.
  • Do not click links in unexpected emails or messages.
  • Use the app or official number if you are unsure.
  • Set transaction alerts so you notice anything unusual.

If something looks wrong

Lock your card in the app and call the 24-hour helpline. The numbers on this demo are placeholders.
