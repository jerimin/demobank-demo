Accounts

How do I open an account?

Choose an account, start the sample form here or visit a branch with your ID and proof of address. In this demo nothing is saved.

What is the minimum balance?

It depends on the account type; the figures on each product page are illustrative for this demo.

How do I close an account?

Visit a branch with your ID. An early-closure charge may apply, as shown on the product page.

Cards

How do I apply for a card?

Open the card you want and use the sample apply button. A real bank would check your details and income.

How do I block a lost card?

Lock it in the app or call the helpline. The numbers here are placeholders.

When is my credit card payment due?

On the date shown on your statement. Paying in full avoids interest in this demo's example terms.

Loans

How much can I borrow?

Each loan page shows an indicative maximum. Your real limit would depend on income and the product.

How is my instalment calculated?

On a reducing balance — interest applies to what you still owe. Try the home-page calculator.

Can I repay early?

Yes; part-prepayment lowers your interest. An early-settlement charge may apply.
